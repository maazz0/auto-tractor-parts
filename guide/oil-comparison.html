Our Honest Verdict After 25 Years

🏆 Best Overall: Shell Rimula R4 X

For MF 375, 385, 392 with turbo engines — Rimula's detergent additives keep turbo bearings clean even in 48°C summer heat. Worth the Rs. 1,400 premium over 20L. We've opened 200+ engines using Rimula since 2002 — piston ring wear is consistently lowest.

Best Value: Delo Gold (Caltex) — Excellent for naturally aspirated MF 240, 260, 285. Performs within 5% of Rimula at lower cost. Our second-most-sold oil for 15 years running.

Best for New Holland: Al Ghazi Genuine — Specifically formulated for NH 480/640/640S engines. Lower fake risk because Al Ghazi controls its own distribution. Change every 200 hours (not 250) due to lower additive package.

How to Spot Fake Oil — 7-Point Checklist

Fake oil is a Rs. 18 billion problem in Pakistan annually. Here's what we check at Naeem Auto before stocking any drum:

  1. Seal integrity: Original Shell has double-layer induction seal with laser batch code. Fake seals are often single-layer with printed (not laser) codes.
  2. Batch code verification: WhatsApp the batch code to Shell Pakistan's official number. They confirm authenticity within minutes.
  3. Cap threading: Original caps have precise threading and click-lock mechanism. Fakes often cross-thread or feel loose.
  4. Label quality: Genuine labels have sharp printing, hologram stickers (on Rimula 20L), and QR codes that scan to product pages.
  5. Oil color & clarity: Pour a sample. Real oil is golden-amber and clear. Fake oil is often cloudy, dark, or has particles.
  6. Smell test: Genuine diesel oil has a mild chemical smell. Fake oil often smells burnt or like kerosene.
  7. Price sanity: If anyone sells Rimula 20L below Rs. 13,500 in May 2026 — it's fake. No exceptions. Margins don't allow deeper discounts.

🚨 Critical Warning

We recently inspected 3 MF 385 engines that failed within 400 hours of oil change. All used oil bought from roadside pumps at "discount prices." Engine rebuild cost: Rs. 85,000–1,20,000 each. Oil savings: Rs. 2,000. Always buy from authorized dealers only.

Oil Change Intervals — Don't Guess, Follow This

  • MF 240, 260 (non-turbo): Every 250 hours or 6 months, whichever comes first
  • MF 285 (turbo): Every 200 hours or 6 months
  • MF 375, 385, 392 (turbocharged): Every 250 hours with Rimula/Delo; 200 hours with other brands
  • Al Ghazi 480, 640, 640S: Every 200 hours with Al Ghazi Genuine oil
  • Heavy-duty use (combine, ploughing in dust): Reduce intervals by 25%

Always change the oil filter with every oil change. A Rs. 600 filter protects your Rs. 16,000 oil investment. We stock genuine MF, Fleetguard, and Baldwin filters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I mix Shell Rimula with Delo Gold?
A: Technically yes in emergency, but not recommended. Different additive packages can react and reduce protection. If mixing is unavoidable, change the oil within 50 hours.
Q: 20W-50 vs 15W-40 — which is better for Punjab summer?
A: 15W-40 (multigrade) is superior. It flows faster at cold start (reducing 70% of engine wear) and maintains viscosity at 48°C. 20W-50 is outdated technology — only use if manufacturer specifically requires it (older Fiat models).
Q: My mechanic says "heavy oil" is better for old engines. True?
A: Myth. Thicker oil (20W-50 or SAE 50) in worn engines increases oil pressure readings temporarily but reduces lubrication to critical parts. Better solution: fix the wear (bearings, pump) and use proper 15W-40.
Q: Do you offer oil change service at the shop?
A: Yes. Complete oil change (drain, new oil, new filter, visual inspection) at our Old Bus Stand Vehari workshop. Labor: Rs. 500. Total time: 30 minutes. No appointment needed Mon–Sun 8AM–10PM.
Q: Is there a difference between "diesel engine oil" and "tractor engine oil"?
A: Yes. Tractor-specific oils (like Al Ghazi Genuine) have additional additives for wet brakes and hydraulic systems that share engine oil in some models. For MF tractors (separate hydraulic system), standard diesel oil (Rimula/Delo) works perfectly.
