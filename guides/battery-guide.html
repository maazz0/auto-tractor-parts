A dead battery during wheat harvest means your tractor won't start at 5 AM — and by the time you get it jump-started, you've lost 3 hours of the coolest working window. In Punjab's summer heat, battery failure is the #1 electrical problem we see at our Vehari workshop.

This guide covers which battery brand actually lasts, what sizes fit your MF or Al Ghazi tractor, and the maintenance habits that take a battery from an 18-month life to 3+ years.

⚠️ Avoid "Dry" or "Solar" Batteries for Tractors

Some dealers sell cheaper "solar batteries" or "UPS batteries" as tractor batteries. These are deep-cycle batteries — they're not designed to deliver the 400+ amps needed for diesel engine starting. They'll work for 3 months, then fail permanently. Only buy automotive starter batteries (marked "SLI").

7 Habits That Extend Battery Life to 3+ Years

  1. Disconnect during long storage. If tractor won't be used for 2+ weeks, disconnect negative terminal.
  2. Clean terminals every 3 months. White/blue corrosion = resistance = poor charging. Use baking soda + water, then apply petroleum jelly.
  3. Check water levels monthly. Use only distilled water. Tap water minerals destroy plates.
  4. Never leave lights on overnight. A single headlight left on for 8 hours can kill a 100Ah battery permanently.
  5. Tighten hold-down clamp. Vibration is the #2 killer of batteries.
  6. Test alternator output yearly. Charging voltage should be 13.8–14.4V.
  7. Avoid frequent short starts. Each start draws 400A. Driving less than 10 minutes doesn't fully recharge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I jump-start my tractor from a car?
A: Yes, but only with a 12V car (not 24V truck). Connect positive to positive, negative to tractor chassis. After starting, run tractor at 1500 RPM for 20 minutes to recharge.
Q: Maintenance-free vs regular battery — which is better?
A: Maintenance-free (sealed) is convenient but shorter-lived in Pakistan's heat (1.5–2 years). Regular batteries with refillable cells last 3+ years IF you top up with distilled water monthly.
Q: Do you buy back old batteries?
A: Yes. Old lead-acid batteries have scrap value. We give Rs. 3,500–5,500 exchange discount on a new battery when you return your old one.
