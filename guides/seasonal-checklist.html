Seasonal · Printable Checklist

Wheat Harvest &
Rice Sowing Prep.

25-point pre-season maintenance checklist. Avoid mid-field breakdowns during the most expensive weeks of your year.

A breakdown during wheat harvest costs you Rs. 25,000–50,000 per day in delayed work, hired replacement tractors, and potential crop loss. Yet 80% of mid-field breakdowns are caused by problems you could have caught in a 2-hour pre-season check.

This printable checklist has been refined over 24 years at our Vehari workshop. Do it 3 weeks before your season starts. If any item needs repair, you still have time to fix it — instead of panicking in the field.

🛢️ Fluids & Filters (Do First)

🛢️
Engine Oil & Filters
✓
Engine oil change — 15W-40 diesel oil (Shell Rimula R4 or Delo Gold). MF 240/260: 8L. MF 285/385: 9.5L.
✓
Oil filter replacement — genuine MF or Fleetguard. Never reuse.
✓
Fuel filter — primary + secondary. Check for water in fuel separator.
✓
Air filter — replace if dusty environment (wheat harvest = very dusty).
✓
Hydraulic oil level — top up with UTTO. Check for milky contamination.
✓
Hydraulic filter — replace if over 800 hours since last change.

❄️ Cooling System (Summer Critical)

❄️
Radiator & Cooling
✓
Coolant level — check when cold. Top up with 50/50 antifreeze mix (never plain water).
✓
Radiator fins — clean with compressed air from engine side outward.
✓
Radiator hoses — replace if soft, cracked, or swollen.
✓
Radiator cap — replace if rubber seal is cracked (Rs. 300–500 part).
✓
Fan belt — 12–15mm deflection when pressed. Replace if cracked.
✓
Water pump — check for coolant leak from weep hole.

⚙️ Drive & Transmission

⚙️
Clutch & Gearbox
✓
Clutch pedal free-play — 25–30mm. Adjust if out of range.
✓
Clutch engagement — test under load. Replace if slipping.
✓
Gearbox oil level — top up with EP-90 gear oil if needed.
✓
Gear shifting — all gears engage smoothly. Grinding = synchro wear.
✓
PTO engagement — test both speeds (540/1000 RPM).

🔧 Hydraulics & Three-Point Hitch

🔧
Lift System
✓
Lift test with load — 500kg minimum. Watch for slow or jerky movement.
✓
Hydraulic hoses — check for leaks, cracks, or rubbing damage.
✓
Lift link pins — greased and not excessively worn.
✓
Drop speed control — adjust if load drops too fast.

🔋 Electrical & Battery

🔋
Starting & Lights
✓
Battery terminals — clean, tight, no corrosion. Apply grease.
✓
Battery water — top up with distilled water if not maintenance-free.
✓
Starter motor — cranks strongly. Slow crank = battery or starter issue.
✓
Alternator — charges battery (voltmeter should show 13.8–14.4V at idle).
✓
All lights — headlights, indicators, brake lights, hazard.

🛞 Tyres, Brakes & Grease Points

🛞
Final Inspection
✓
Tyre pressure — rear: 12–14 psi for field work, 18–20 psi for road.
✓
Wheel nuts — torque-checked (especially after recent puncture).
✓
Brakes — both sides engage evenly. Adjust if pulling to one side.
✓
Grease all nipples — front axle, steering joints, lift links, PTO shaft.
✓
All bolts — check tightness on engine mounts, fenders, drawbar.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need to do ALL 25 points every season?
A: Yes, but some take 30 seconds (check lights, tyre pressure) and some take 10 minutes (oil change). Total DIY time: 2–3 hours. The Rs. 9,999 package completes it all in 4 hours with genuine parts.
Q: I used my tractor last season and it worked fine. Do I still need this?
A: Yes. Parts wear gradually. A filter that was "fine" last season is likely 80% clogged now. A belt that was "acceptable" can snap mid-harvest. Prevention is 10x cheaper than repair.
Q: Can I bring my tractor in the evening after field work?
A: Yes. We're open until 10 PM Mon–Sun. Drop off in the evening, collect next morning. Call ahead to reserve your slot during peak season.
Q: Do you service combine harvesters and rotavators too?
A: Yes — PTO-driven implements (rotavators, threshers, cultivators) can be inspected with your tractor for Rs. 2,000 extra. Combine harvesters need separate specialist service — we can refer trusted mechanics in Vehari.
Q: Can I print this checklist for my farm?
A: Yes. Use your browser's print function (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P). Select "Save as PDF" for mobile access. We also hand out printed copies at the shop — free, no purchase required.
